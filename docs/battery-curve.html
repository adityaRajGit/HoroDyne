Reading it correctly

  • Match against a resting cell. Measure with the screen off and the charger out, after a few minutes. The percent only moves on resting readings while on battery.
  • The middle is flat. From 10% to 55% the cell moves only 0.16 V, so a 0.01 V meter or divider error shifts the percent by about 3 points.
  • On USB the reading is not the cell. Charge current lifts it, so the watch subtracts an offset: 0.30 V by default, 0.35 V measured on this cell on 2026-10-26, and relearned each time you unplug.
  • Near full, voltage stops helping. At 4.15 V and above on the charger, percent rises 1 point every 90 s, stops at 99% until unplugged, and never falls while charging.
  • This is a generic curve. The table is a typical single-cell LiPo discharge curve and has not been measured on your 802540 cell. A worn cell will differ, so a long unplugged log could replace it with your own numbers.
